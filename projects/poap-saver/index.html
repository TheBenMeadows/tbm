POAP Saver

POAP shut down in August 2026. Your badges are permanent tokens on Gnosis Chain, but their names, descriptions, and artwork live on POAP’s servers, and the pointer written into each token cannot be changed. When those servers go dark, that layer is gone.

This page saves it while they still answer. Enter an address or ENS name; your browser fetches every badge’s metadata and artwork directly, records a SHA-256 of each image, and gives you one zip: the archive plus a gallery page that works offline, forever. Nothing is uploaded, and no wallet connection is ever asked for — an address is all it needs.

Saving also feeds a community mirror, which keeps a copy of each badge’s artwork so it stays reachable after POAP’s servers stop answering. The mirror never receives your files: it fetches artwork from POAP itself and checks it against POAP’s own metadata. It learns which events were saved, and your IP address — not your wallet address. Every badge it holds is already public on chain. Uncheck the box to skip it, and the page talks to nobody but POAP and a public Gnosis node.

    Runs entirely in your browser against public RPC endpoints and POAP’s own hosts. Large collections take a few minutes and are held in memory until the zip downloads. The same tool exists for the command line, and both are open source: github.com/mdws-org/poap-saver. The collection that started this is at /experiments/poap-vault/.
