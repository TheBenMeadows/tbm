Marfa

I am showing at Plastic Cactus, a group exhibition of independent artists, 22 to 24 October 2026 at 301 W Dallas St, Marfa, Texas, during Art Blocks Marfa Weekend. I have come to Marfa Weekend since 2024. This is my first time showing there.

On the wall

One screen runs essentialism live: the same generator that is stored in the Tezos contract, drawing a new seed every half minute. A second screen rotates through the rest of the work, photographs and on-chain pieces from 2022 on.

Beside the screens are ten archival A4 prints of Blue Tree, the photograph the generator on the screen is forgetting. They are free. If you have a Tezos wallet on your phone, I will mint an essentialism edition for you on the spot as well; if not, the print is yours anyway. The edition is 44, and 36 remain as I write this.

Bio

I started with film cameras and moved to digital in the late 90s. I have been manipulating images digitally for almost thirty years, and I sold my first photographs in 2016. Most of what I shoot is Arizona, a lot of it from rooftops.

I have been experimenting with blockchain since 2011 and minted my first piece in 2022. I like being early to technology, and the work has spread out with it: photography, AI-assisted pieces, glitch and abstract work, and fully on-chain pieces on Ethereum, Bitcoin and Tezos. In 2023, I worked out a way to store and view compressed vector art as Bitcoin inscriptions. In 2026, I released essentialism, a generative system stored fully on-chain on Tezos. I'm currently working on my most ambitious generative system yet. Some of my work has left the screen, as Bitcoin-backed prints with Gamma and in shows in Barcelona, Paris, Ghent, Bologna, Lisbon, Bali, Las Vegas, and more. I also curated ABC Art World on Foundation, and I collect: thousands of pieces across ETH, BTC, SOL and Tezos.
