Save My POAPs

POAP shut down in August 2026. Each badge below is a permanent token on Gnosis Chain, but its name, description, and artwork lived on POAP’s servers, and the pointer to them is written into the token and cannot be changed. Before those servers went dark, every badge in our wallets was archived: image and metadata saved, each image checked against a SHA-256 of exactly what POAP served.

357 badges · archived 2026-08-04 · contract 0x22C1…B415 on Gnosis and Ethereum

The grid shows 400px WebP copies made for the web. Opening a badge loads the original, full animations included, from the IPFS archive, hash-checked against the copy saved at rescue time.
