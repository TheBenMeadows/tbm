Birds Planner

Birds by Jules and Mungi is a network of 100 birds moving between home, five sanctuaries and away. Observing a bird in a sanctuary mints a record of that pair, and there are 500 pairs. Every observation also moves the whole world: the layout of all five sanctuaries is redrawn at once. This page shows which pairs a wallet still needs, who is in each sanctuary right now, and who will be there if an observation lands in the next block.

      How the forecast works

      Each observation starts a new epoch with a seed of keccak256(previous block hash, observations contract, epoch). The seed picks one of 2,800 orderings of the 100 birds: a multiplier from a fixed list of 28 values and an offset from 100. Sanctuary n takes slot 29 + n of that ordering. The previous block’s hash is public before the next block is built, so the arrangement an observation will produce is known one block in advance. Checked against the last 400 observations on chain: 399 of them have a following observation to compare with, and all 399 matched.

      That makes a full set possible with no wasted observations: send only into a block whose arrangement still holds a pair you need. A browser wallet cannot pick its block, so on this page the forecast is a guide rather than a guarantee.
