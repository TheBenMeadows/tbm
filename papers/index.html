Information Retrieval 02

A Score Ceiling in Reciprocal Rank Fusion Detects Zero Overlap, and Found a Dead Retriever

Reciprocal rank fusion gives a document found by one retriever a score of at most 1/k, so a result list that never rises above 1/k holds nothing both retrievers returned. That ceiling showed a keyword retriever that had been returning nothing for most queries. The paper derives the ceiling and reproduces the failure on two BEIR collections.
