**"Intro to Blockchain, NFTs & Web3"**
July 1, 2022 - In-Person Event - Host: [Club FOMO](https://x.com/theclubfomo)

I was asked to do a 90-minute informational session on Blockchain, NFTs & Web3 for Club FOMO (billed as "the first-ever real-world NFT club") for their inaugural Crypto Curious: First Friday event in Phoenix, Arizona.

The point of this presentation was to share my knowledge of blockchain technology from almost a decade of experience in the industry, and distill it down in such a way that its significant complexities could be easily understood by newcomers to the space.

- [Watch the Recording](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PL-caOdqO4A8ffp9jer5OCxodxzs97ynpe&si=OwcUEwhsTnDcM4Mu)

**"IT Hardware & Systems / Network Security"**
May 2019 - Keynote Speaker - National Conference (PHCC)

In May 2019, as a featured conference speaker, I gave a 90-minute presentation on IT Hardware & Systems / Network Security at PHCC Construction Contractors' Alliance's national meeting in Madison, Wisconsin.

This conference brought together some of the largest specialty contracting companies in the country to talk about best practices related to technology, and was a great opportunity for me to share what I had learned while running the Technology Department for a medium-sized company for several years.
